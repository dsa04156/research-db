## 요약·초록

What is Kubernetes? Why is it called k8s? What makes it so popular? Let’s take a look. Kubernetes is an open-source container orchestration platform. It automates the deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications. Kubernetes can be traced back to Google's internal container orchestration system, Borg, which managed the deployment of thousands of applications within Google. In 2014, Google open-sourced a version of Borg. That is Kubernetes. Why is it called k8s? This is a somewhat nerdy way of abbreviating long words.
