## 요약·초록

The fast digitalization of the new business has already enhanced the attribution of scalable, secure and smart integration designs, especially in the very regulated sectors of finance and healthcare. Such cloud-first approaches, coupled with recent developments in artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) have helped organizations to modernize old systems, become operationally agile, and make evidence-based decisions. The paper will introduce an Azulike AI/ML-based orchestrated workflow infrastructure to assist in a smooth integration of the enterprise in a heterogeneous system in financial and healthcare settings. The suggested framework uses fundamental Azure services, such as Azure Logic Apps, Azure Data Factory, Azure Machine Learning, Azure functions and Azure Kubernetes service (AKS) to facilitate intelligent automation, real-time analytics, predictive decision support, and policy-driven workflow management. The paper discusses the architectural design, workflow coordination, AI/ML integration approach, and compliance and security aspects of the work in the context of regulated enterprises in a systematic manner. Performance, scalability, resilience, and performance compliance preparedness Domain-specific financial and healthcare systems use cases are used to test the systems. The results show that the Azure-native AI/ML-driven orchestrated workflows can greatly improve interoperability, scalability, fault tolerance, and regulatory compliance and lower integration complexities and operational costs at the same time. In general, the research study shows how intelligent and cloud-first orchestration frameworks can revolutionize enterprise integration in data-intensive and regulation sensitive industries.
