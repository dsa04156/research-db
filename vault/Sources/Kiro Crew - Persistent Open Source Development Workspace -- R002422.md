## 요약·초록

Kiro's official page describes Kiro Crew as an open-source development workspace that persists sessions and memory, turns corrections into editable lessons and skills, runs scheduled or webhook-triggered work, coordinates multiple agents through ACP, and keeps orchestration visible through approvals and signed audit logs. The product itself is free software but model reasoning consumes a Kiro plan; scheduled script-only jobs do not consume model requests.
