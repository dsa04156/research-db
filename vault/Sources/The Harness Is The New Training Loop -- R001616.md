## 요약·초록

Before models rewrite themselves, they are probably going to rewrite the software around them. That sounds less dramatic than recursive self-improvement. It is also much closer to what the papers are actually showing. When people talk about self-improving AI, the mental image is usually a model cracking open its own weights, changing itself, becoming smarter, and then doing it again. Big sci-fi energy. [laughter] But the near-term version is more boring. In the best engineering sense, you have a model around it. You have context, assembly, tools, files, traces, retries, permissions, tests, evolves, memory, sub agents, and all the glue code that decides what the model sees and what it is allowed to do.
