## 요약·초록

This chapter examines the evolution of conversational marketing from rule-based chatbots to generative AI and autonomous agents capable of inference, persuasion, optimization, and market-facing execution. It argues that the rise of agentic marketing requires a shift from adoption-centered analysis to a governance-centered perspective that connects personalization, trust, control, and accountability. Drawing on marketing, information systems, communication, organization studies, law, and ethics, the chapter develops the AGTC framework to explain how agency, governance, trust, and control interact across platform, firm, and consumer levels. It also identifies theoretical, methodological, and contextual gaps, especially in emerging markets, multilingual environments, and uneven regulatory settings, while outlining implications for research, managerial strategy, and public policy in the age of autonomous market systems.
