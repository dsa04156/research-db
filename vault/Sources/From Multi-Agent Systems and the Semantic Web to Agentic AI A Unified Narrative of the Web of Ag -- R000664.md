## 요약·초록

Supplementary material accompanying the manuscript "From Multi-Agent Systems and the Semantic Web to Agentic AI: A Unified Narrative of the Web of Agents" (Petrova, Bliznioukov, Puzikov, State; 2026; arXiv:2507.10644). This deposit supports the reproducibility statements in Sections 1.2 and 1.2 (Reproducibility) and Figure 2 caption of the manuscript. Contents: (i) References and screening- references.bib — full BibTeX bibliography (201 entries: 155 cited in the manuscript + 46 screened-out candidates retained from the working set).- bibliography_inclusion_log.csv — per-entry inclusion/exclusion log for all 201 candidates with categorised rationale (INCLUDED, CONSIDERED, REDUNDANT, REPLACED, DUPLICATE, REMOVED_WITH_FIGURE, OUT_OF_SCOPE, REMOVED_WITH_SENTENCE). (ii) OpenAlex bibliometric data underlying Figure 2- openalex_queries.csv — 9 queries (3 primary + 6 triangulation; 2 alternative queries per generation).- openalex_counts.csv — raw per-year counts (1995–2026) for each of the 9 queries plus the CS-output normalisation denominator.- openalex_normalized.csv — per-100,000-CS normalised values.- run_openalex_queries.py — re-runnable Python script (standard library only; polite-pool email; ~320 API calls, ~2 minutes). (iii) TikZ figure sources- figures/fig1_timeline_3gen.tex — Figure 1 (three-generation chronology + 2024–2026 institutional zoom).- figures/fig2_pub_volume.tex — Figure 2 (publication-share trajectory; analytic window 2002–2026; pre-2002 dashed).- figures/fig3_prisma.tex — Figure 3 (PRISMA-style selection funnel).- figures/fig4_paradigm_shift.tex — Figure 4 (Semantic-Effort Migration).- figures/fig5_lessons_challenges.tex — Figure 5 (7×7 Lessons–Challenges pairing matrix). Full methodology, including the pre-2002 OpenAlex coverage-gap handling and the triangulation robustness check, is documented in README.md. Manuscript permanent identifier: arXiv:2507.10644.
