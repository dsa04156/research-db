## 요약·초록

This study addresses the limitations of existing remote and portable systems for patient health monitoring, which often lack flexibility and ease of data interpretation. Leveraging recent advancements in information technology and cloud computing, a novel Remote Patient Monitoring System (RPMS) has been developed based on the Medicine 4.0 paradigm, emphasizing smart healthcare solutions. The proposed RPMS is lightweight and wearable, allowing patients to carry on with daily activities seamlessly. It incorporates microcontroller-based medical sensors that transmit real-time health data via IoT connections to the cloud, where it is processed and visualized for medical personnel using Amazon Web Services (AWS). Experimental evaluations demonstrated a reduction in data transmission delay by 32% compared to conventional systems, and the system achieved a real-time alert accuracy of 96.7%. These results highlight the system’s efficiency in timely detection and response to critical health events. Furthermore, the design allows future integration with artificial intelligence (AI) for advanced diagnostics. The system is recommended for use by clinics, emergency medical services, and military health units for real-time, field-level patient monitoring.
