## 요약·초록

The rising frequency and sophistication of cyberattacks—ranging from nation-state espionage to ransomware targeting critical infrastructure—demands a radical transformation in how national cyber defense is architected, deployed, and maintained. Traditional perimeter-based systems, isolated Security Operations Centers (SOCs), and legacy incident response protocols have proven inadequate against distributed, AI-enabled, and zero-day threats. In response, this paper presents NCAF (National Cybersecurity Architecture Framework), a federated, AI-powered, Zero-Trust–driven security framework tailored for the protection of government agencies, critical infrastructure, and cross-sector organizations. NCAF introduces a Level-5 (L5) architectural blueprint, spanning five abstraction layers: national governance, intelligent analytics, policy orchestration, federated infrastructure, and endpoint execution. We combine modern Zero Trust principles (NIST SP 800-207), secure DevSecMLOps pipelines, real-time AI-based threat detection, and infrastructure-as-code deployment to build a reproducible and scalable defense platform. The system enables secure telemetry sharing between sectors, supports SBOM compliance (as mandated by Executive Order 14028), and integrates a multi-cloud–ready policy engine to adapt rapidly to evolving threats. Our implementation includes reference code in Python, Go, Rust, and Terraform, with eBPF-based edge sensors and a Kubernetes-native inference engine. Evaluation using real-world ICS/OT simulations and open attack datasets shows ≥96% detection recall, sub-second threat classification latency, and robust policy enforcement across distributed environments. This framework aims to advance the state of practice in national cyber defense by offering a flexible, reproducible, and standards-aligned approach suitable for both academic and operational deployments.
