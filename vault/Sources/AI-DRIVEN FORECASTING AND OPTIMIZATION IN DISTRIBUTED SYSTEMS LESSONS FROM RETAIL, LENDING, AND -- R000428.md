## 요약·초록

Distributed systems underpin modern digital platforms across retail, financial services, and healthcare.As these systems scale, they face increasing volatility in demand, heterogeneous workloads, and stringent performance and reliability requirements.Traditional rule-based capacity planning and reactive optimization approaches are insufficient in environments characterized by real-time data flows and complex interdependencies.This paper presents a generalized, non-proprietary analysis of AI-driven forecasting and optimization techniques applied to distributed systems.Drawing lessons from retail, lending, and healthcare platforms, the study explores how machine learning-based demand forecasting, predictive resource optimization, and closed-loop control mechanisms enhance system resilience, cost efficiency, and service quality.A reference architecture, comparative analysis, and illustrative use cases are provided to guide practitioners in adopting AI-enabled operational intelligence.
