## 요약·초록

The rapid acceleration of cloud-based container adoption has fundamentally reshaped how enterprises build and deploy applications. This chapter dives into the emerging trends, evolving technologies, and strategic practices that will define the future of container security. It aims to provide architects, security leaders, and decision-makers with a clear, actionable perspective on securing cloud-native ecosystems in the years ahead. Container orchestration platforms such as Kubernetes have redefined how organizations manage distributed workloads at scale. Looking ahead, orchestration tools will evolve from operational utilities into intelligent security enablers. Zero Trust architecture will take center stage in container security strategies, particularly given the dynamic and ephemeral nature of containerized workloads. Service mesh technologies such as Istio and Linkerd are rapidly becoming enablers of Zero Trust within container ecosystems. Future security practices will deeply integrate image scanning and compliance validation into CI/CD pipelines, creating automated feedback loops for developers.
