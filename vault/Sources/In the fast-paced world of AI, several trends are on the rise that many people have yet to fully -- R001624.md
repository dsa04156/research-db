## 요약·초록

In the fast-paced world of AI, several trends are on the rise that many people have yet to fully understand. One such trend is the fusion of AI with edge computing. Traditionally, AI relied on cloud-based systems, but edge computing shifts AI processing closer to the data source. This allows for real-time data analysis and decision-making, which is crucial for technologies like autonomous vehicles and smart devices. Another area that is not fully appreciated is AI ethics and governance. With AI systems becoming more ubiquitous, addressing ethical issues like bias and transparency is increasingly important.
