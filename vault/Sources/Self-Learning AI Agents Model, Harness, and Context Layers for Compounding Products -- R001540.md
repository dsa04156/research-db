## 요약·초록

TL;DR: Most AI agents forget every correction the moment a session ends. A better architecture captures two signals — agent traces and human corrections — from the same interface, routes lessons into the layers you actually control (harness + context, not model weights), sorts them into semantic/episodic/procedural memory, and scopes them per-user/team/app so data doesn&#39;t leak across customers
