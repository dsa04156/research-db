## 요약·초록

The Semantic Fidelity Paper Series examines how AI systems preserve meaning across memory, retrieval, monitoring, and changing real-world conditions.This collection contains five papers connecting current work on AI agents, long-term memory, model governance, concept drift, context engineering, and prompt design to the broader concepts of Semantic Fidelity and Reality Drift. Together, the papers examine how systems can remain technically functional while stored context, internal representations, governance procedures, or monitoring signals gradually lose alignment with what they are intended to represent.Included papers:AI Agents, Long-Term Memory, and Semantic FidelityAI Agents, Memory, and Semantic FidelityAI Governance, Model Monitoring, and Reality DriftConcept Drift, Model Drift, Reality Drift, and Machine LearningContext Engineering, Prompt Engineering, and Semantic Fidelity
