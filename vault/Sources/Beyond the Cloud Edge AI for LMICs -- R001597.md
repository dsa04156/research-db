## 요약·초록

A GSMA research report on edge AI deployment in low- and middle-income countries, based on AI-capable smartphone forecasts across 11 markets, cloud-versus-edge cost modelling, more than 150 deployment mappings, and sector case studies. It treats edge inference as a workload-specific alternative where connectivity, bandwidth cost, privacy, and local compute constraints matter.
