## 요약·초록

Abstract Offshore and Deepwater operations are a key part of the energy supply, yet they remain highly energy intensive and carbon intensive due to their dependence on fuel gas- and diesel-powered systems. As sustainability expectations continue to rise, offshore operators are under growing pressure to maintain reliable production while reducing emissions, improving efficiency, and strengthening operational reliability. Most offshore power systems are built around centralized gas turbines and diesel generators. While these systems are proven and reliable, they offer limited flexibility and are not well suited for integrating renewable energy sources. They are also associated with high fuel consumption, increased maintenance costs, higher emissions, and occasional operational downtime. Limited space on offshore platforms further restricts the addition of new power equipment and alternative energy solutions. This paper discusses the application of hybrid energy systems for offshore and Deepwater operations by combining offshore wind energy, solar photovoltaics, and energy storage with existing conventional power systems. Rather than adding significant new equipment to already crowded platforms, the hybrid approach relies on power generated away from the platform, compact modular systems, and smart energy management to overcome space and integration challenges. Artificial intelligence enabled energy management systems are introduced as decision support tools that balance power generation, storage, and consumption based on operational demand and system constraints. AI is positioned as an enabler that improves coordination, efficiency, and reliability across the hybrid energy system. A simple framework shows how hybrid energy systems can be applied throughout the life cycle of an offshore asset, from early design to daily operations. The framework emphasizes collaboration between energy, engineering, and digital teams. Expected benefits include reduced fuel gas use, lower emissions, improved power reliability, and greater operational flexibility. Hybrid energy systems offer a pathway for offshore assets to support the energy transition without compromising energy security.
