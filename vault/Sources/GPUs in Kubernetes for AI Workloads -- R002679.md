## 요약·초록

Here is a question. Where do we run AI models? Everyone knows the answer to this question. We run them on servers with graphics processing units (GPUs). GPUs are much more efficient at processing AI models, or more precisely, inference. But here's another question. How do I manage models on these servers? The answer to this question is Kubernetes. Kubernetes is the de facto standard for managing any type of workload. Be it stateless applications, stateful applications, tasks, AI models, or anything else.
