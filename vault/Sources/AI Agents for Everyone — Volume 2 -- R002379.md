## 요약·초록

AI Agents for Everyone: Volume 2 — How AI Agents Work, Collaborate, and Act is a beginner-friendly and self-contained introduction to the rapidly evolving world of AI agents and multi-agent systems. Written for students, professionals, educators, entrepreneurs, and readers without a technical AI background, the book builds a continuous path from the foundations of generative AI and large language models to systems capable of planning, using tools, accessing external knowledge, maintaining memory, and taking actions toward a goal. The book explains how language models become AI agents; how retrieval-augmented generation, memory, tools, permissions, and execution systems work together; and how modern AI systems such as ChatGPT and Claude can move from conversation to multi-step action. It also examines Anthropic's published work on Claude's research and coding architectures to illustrate when a single-agent approach is preferable and when work benefits from being divided among multiple agents. A dedicated section introduces multi-agent systems through intuitive collaboration patterns, including sequential pipelines, router-based systems, coordinated parallel teams, agent handoffs, and human-augmented workflows. Practical examples show how these patterns can be applied while controlling complexity, cost, context, permissions, and human approval. The final part addresses responsible AI agents, evaluation, readiness, governance, human oversight, and the evidence required before increasingly autonomous systems are trusted with consequential actions. The volume includes practical examples, conceptual diagrams, key takeaways, a glossary, an index, and sources for further reading. No prior AI or machine-learning background is required.
