## 요약·초록

Proof-of-concept artifact of the master's thesis "Designing Hybrid Intelligence for Knowledge Application: A DSR Approach in the Context of End-User Development" (FOM Hochschule für Oekonomie und Management, Cologne, 2026). Generative AI reshapes knowledge-work. Yet knowledge application as situated enactment of existing knowledge is the process on which performance ultimately depends while its barriers remain hardly addressed. This work addresses a knowledge-application gap by design aiming for hybrid intelligence. Following a design science research approach an end-user configurable Hybrid Knowledge-Application System (HKAS) is constructed and evaluated as a proof of concept with 15 participants. It uses a transactive-memory extension for intelligent agents as a declared derivation template for the individual (1+n) human–AI constellation that is based on a shared memory store and three AI agent roles. A within-subject ablation study contrasts a baseline without AI assistance (C1) with a monolithic AI tool (C2) and a transactive actor structure (C3). The findings are a double dissociation: application quality rises from 66.1 to 77.6 and 78.0 %, an exploratory effect carried by the tool contrast, while the actor structure adds no measurable further performance. Preference reverses this finding as eleven of 15 participants rank the actor structure first. Performance comes from access to knowledge but acceptance from the visible actor structure, which participants read as a trust signal. The knowledge-application gap is narrowed and transformed but not closed. Contents of this archive. The evaluated artifact as used in the field phase (July 2026), the frozen memory store with its field-phase embeddings (52 concepts, 102 author-curated typed edges; gemini-embedding-001, 3072 dimensions), and the analysis pipeline with the pseudonymized study data (R pipeline, per-participant scores, both rating states, blind re-rating subsample, generated figures and statistics). Not included. Participants' free-text responses are deliberately withheld: with N = 15 from a small professional environment, wording and context can identify individuals, and the consent obtained does not cover publishing raw responses. Only the short excerpts quoted in the thesis are reproduced. This is a frozen research artifact documenting the system as it was evaluated, not a maintained product.
