## 요약·초록

Meta introduced Muse, a personal agent that runs in a persistent dedicated VM with a browser, works across connected apps, continues tasks in the background, and requires approval for critical actions such as messages and purchases. Meta says credentials are kept in a separate store that the agent cannot read and that users can inspect an audit trail. Availability and safety claims are vendor-reported.
