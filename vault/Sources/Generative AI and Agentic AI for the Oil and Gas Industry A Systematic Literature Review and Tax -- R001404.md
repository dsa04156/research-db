## 요약·초록

The oil and gas industry is rapidly adopting advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to enhance operational efficiency, improve decision-making, strengthen safety, and accelerate digital transformation.While conventional AI and machine learning have been extensively applied to tasks such as reservoir characterization, predictive maintenance, and production optimization, the emergence of Generative AI (GenAI) and Agentic AI represents a significant shift toward intelligent and autonomous industrial systems.However, existing research remains fragmented, with limited comprehensive reviews examining their applications, industrial adoption, and future potential across the oil and gas value chain.This paper presents a systematic literature review to synthesize recent advances in Generative AI and Agentic AI for the oil and gas industry.Publications from major scientific databases are systematically analyzed to identify research trends, enabling technologies, application domains, and implementation challenges.Based on the reviewed literature, the paper proposes a comprehensive taxonomy of industrial use cases spanning upstream, midstream, downstream, and enterprise operations, including exploration, drilling, production, refining, asset management, health, safety and environment (HSE), supply chain, and sustainability.The review further examines enabling technologies such as Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, digital twins, and multi-agent systems, while highlighting key challenges related to data governance, explainability, cybersecurity, and integration with operational technology.The findings provide a comprehensive foundation for researchers and industry practitioners to advance AI-enabled digital transformation and autonomous operations across the oil and gas industry.
