## 요약·초록

Self-improving agents just hit state of the art by evolving their own harness. One problem: dumb retries beat it at the same compute budget, and on unseen tasks the gain was +0.6. Neural Architecture Search was the same pattern. Search that evaluates itself ends up as p-hacking. Two questions for every "self-improving agent" paper: Same budget as retries? Held-out tasks? Paper: Rethinking the Evaluation of Harness Evolution for Agents (Ai2/UW)
