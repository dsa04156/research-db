## 요약·초록

Official launch announcement for Modelplane, an Apache-2.0 control plane that treats Kubernetes GPU clusters across cloud, neocloud, and on-premises as one inference fleet. It separates fleet-level model placement, routing, autoscaling, and weight caching from each cluster's local scheduler and DRA mechanisms.
