## 요약·초록

In this episode of KubeFm, I spoke with Federico Yetzi from Google Cloud about what it actually takes to serve open source language models at very high throughput on Kubernetes. We use his work on reaching 1 million output tokens per second with QN 3.5, vLLM, GKE Autopilot, and Nvidia B200 GPUs as a starting point. Federico explained why inference performance is often constrained by memory capacity and bandwidth rather than raw compute, how model architecture and KV cache configuration affect throughput, and why representative benchmarking matters more than producing an impressive headline number.
