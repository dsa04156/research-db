## 요약·초록

This review investigates the integration of machine learning-optimised unmanned aerial vehicle systems within the Industry 4.0 paradigm for total quality management and quality assurance of functional surfaces, with particular emphasis on analysing multi-physics interactions including heat transfer, corrosion kinetics, thermo-mechanical coupling, and surface wettability. The review systematically examines how drones function as mobile cyber-physical nodes, equipped with thermal, LiDAR, hyperspectral, and visible imaging sensors, that capture high-fidelity spatial-temporal data from industrial assets and surfaces. It critically evaluates deep learning architectures, including convolutional neural networks for real-time defect detection, physics-informed neural networks for simulating transport phenomena, and reinforcement learning for autonomous navigation and inspection path optimisation. The analysis encompasses the technological enablers spanning edge AI hardware, multimodal sensor fusion techniques, 5G communication infrastructure, and digital twin integration that transform raw sensor data into actionable quality intelligence. The review identifies significant applications across predictive maintenance, automated compliance auditing, smart inventory management, and process quality monitoring, while systematically addressing key challenges, including data scarcity for rare surface defects, model generalisation across heterogeneous materials, emissivity uncertainty in thermal measurements, atmospheric interference, edge computing constraints, and regulatory frameworks for autonomous flight. Furthermore, it outlines future research directions encompassing collaborative drone swarms with multi-agent reinforcement learning, self-learning quality systems employing few-shot learning, advanced hyperspectral thermal sensors, and explainable AI frameworks for regulatory acceptance, ultimately positioning ML-enhanced drones as transformative tools for investigating and managing surface transport phenomena that determine product performance, asset longevity, and operational reliability in smart manufacturing environments.
