## 요약·초록

This paper discusses the technological shift between the legacy systems to the software-defined cloud based on AI architecture. Putting literature reviews and empirical evidence together, we can analyse the way that artificial intelligence can improve the work with resources, security, scalability, and energy consumption in contemporary cloud computing conditions. Research points at the application of AI in cloud migration, edge integration, and intelligent data centres. The quantitative assessments and case studies confirm that the operational agility, latency, and sustainability are improved greatly. The report highlights the disruption factor of AI throughout the cloud-to-edge spectrum and gives strategic input to companies that desire resilient, intelligent and future-proof digital infrastructures.
